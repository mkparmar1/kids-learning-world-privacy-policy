Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 9, 2026
Your privacy matters. Kids Learning World is designed to provide children and families with a safe, fun, and educational experience while minimizing the amount of personal information collected.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Kids Learning World ("we", "our", or "the App").

Kids Learning World is an educational game designed for children and families. The App provides interactive learning activities, games, puzzles, stories, music, animations, and educational content.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Kids Learning World.

2. Children's Privacy

Kids Learning World is designed for children and families.

We do not intentionally collect personal information from children through the App.

Children can use the core learning features without creating an account or providing personal information such as an email address, phone number, home address, or precise location.

We encourage parents and guardians to supervise children's use of the App.

3. Information We Collect

Personal Information

Kids Learning World does not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Precise location
  • Photographs
  • Videos
  • Contacts
  • Passwords
  • Financial information

Learning Progress

The App may store learning and gameplay information locally on the device, including:

  • Completed lessons
  • Stars
  • Coins
  • XP
  • Achievements
  • Stickers
  • Learning progress
  • Daily streaks
  • Game settings
  • Audio preferences
  • Accessibility preferences

This information is used to provide the App's learning and gameplay functionality.

4. Local Storage

Kids Learning World may store learning progress and preferences locally on the user's device.

This may include:

  • Game progress
  • Achievement progress
  • Rewards
  • Settings
  • Child profile information entered by a parent
  • Accessibility preferences
  • Audio preferences

This locally stored information is not intentionally transmitted to us.

If future versions introduce online accounts, cloud synchronization, or online backup, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain those changes.

5. Parent-Provided Information

The App may allow a parent or guardian to create a child profile.

A parent may choose to provide information such as:

  • First name or nickname
  • Age group
  • Avatar
  • Learning preferences

Parents should avoid entering unnecessary personal information. We recommend using a nickname instead of a child's full legal name.

6. Microphone and Camera

The core version of Kids Learning World does not require access to:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • Precise location

If future versions introduce voice recording, camera features, or similar functionality, our privacy practices will be updated accordingly.

7. Advertising

Kids Learning World does not currently display personalized advertising to children.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, advertising services will be reviewed for compliance with applicable children's privacy requirements and Google Play Families policies.

Important This policy must be updated if advertising, advertising SDKs, or other third-party services are introduced into the App.

8. Third-Party Services

The current core learning experience is designed to minimize the use of third-party services that collect personal information.

If third-party services or SDKs are introduced, we will review their data practices and update this Privacy Policy when required.

9. Analytics

The current privacy-first version of Kids Learning World does not intentionally collect personal information from children for analytics purposes.

If analytics services are introduced in a future version, they will be implemented with children's privacy and applicable laws in mind.

10. Notifications

If notifications are enabled, they may be used for purposes such as:

  • Learning reminders
  • Daily challenges
  • Streak reminders
  • Parent reminders

Notification preferences can be managed through the App and/or device settings, where supported.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App.

Because learning progress may be stored locally on the device, users should also protect their Android device using appropriate device security measures.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Learning progress stored locally can generally be removed by:

  • Using available reset or delete options within the App
  • Clearing the App's storage/data through Android settings
  • Uninstalling the App

If personal information is collected through an online service in a future version, appropriate data retention and deletion procedures will be described in an updated Privacy Policy.

13. Parents' Rights

Parents and legal guardians may contact us with questions or requests regarding children's privacy and information practices.

Parents may contact us to ask:

  • What information the App collects
  • How information is used
  • Whether information is shared
  • How information can be deleted
  • How to prevent future collection or use

We will handle privacy requests in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

14. International Privacy

Kids Learning World may be available to users in different countries.

We aim to comply with applicable privacy and children's data protection requirements, including applicable requirements relating to children's privacy and data protection laws.

The exact rights available to users may depend on their location and applicable law.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when:

  • New features are introduced
  • Our data practices change
  • Legal requirements change
  • Third-party services are added
  • App functionality changes

When changes are made, we will update the Last Updated date at the top of this Privacy Policy.
